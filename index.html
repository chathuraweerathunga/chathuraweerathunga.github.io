Approach

Most products are built in pieces. The code by one team, the brand by another, the plan by a third. I do all three together, so the product, the company and the story behind it point in the same direction.

Engineer first.Every venture starts with something real that works. I design, build and ship it myself.

Brand from day one.Identity, content and social presence grow with the product, not after it.

Business in every decision.Technical choices are made against the plan, the market and the people it serves.
